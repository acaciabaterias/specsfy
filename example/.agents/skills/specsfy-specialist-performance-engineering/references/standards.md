# Padrões e referências de performance

## Métricas

- Web: LCP, INP, CLS e recursos críticos.
- API: latency percentiles, throughput, errors e saturation.
- Jobs: queue age, duration, retries e completion rate.
- Banco: execution time, buffers, locks e rows.
